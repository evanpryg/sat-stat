SIMULATOR 2 • Fair-share candies

Five friends have different numbers of candies. Use +/− to change them, then press Share equally.

Five friends pool all their candies into one bag, then split the bag equally. The amount each person gets is the mean.

So the recipe is:

Mean = (sum of all values) ÷ n

Example: 2 + 7 + 3 + 8 + 5 = 25 candies, shared by 5 friends → 25 ÷ 5 = 5 each.

Notice: nobody had to start with exactly 5. The mean does not have to be one of the data values (it can even be a decimal like 5.4).

Singkatnya: Mean (rata-rata) = jumlah semua data ÷ banyak data. Bayangkan permen dikumpulkan lalu dibagi rata.

Data: 2, 4, 9 → mean = 15 ÷ 3 = 5

Put each value on a seesaw like a child sitting at that spot. The mean is where you put the triangle so the seesaw stays perfectly level.

The total distance on the left always equals the total distance on the right.

Why this matters

A child sitting very far from the middle has a lot of “pull.” Move the 9 to 30 and the balance point must move far to the right (mean becomes 12).

👉 That is why the mean is sensitive to extreme values. Remember this; it comes back again and again.

Singkatnya: Mean adalah titik keseimbangan (seperti jungkat-jungkit). Nilai yang sangat jauh akan “menarik” mean ke arahnya.

📏 Spread: how different are the values?

Two things can have the same average and still be completely different. Spread tells us how consistent or predictable data is.

RangeQuartiles & IQRMADStandard deviation
📏
2

Travel times to school (minutes) over 6 days. Both routes average exactly 30 minutes.

🚌 Route A

28, 29, 30, 30, 31, 32
Always about 30. Predictable.

🚗 Route B

15, 20, 30, 35, 40, 40
Sometimes fast, sometimes 40 min. Risky!

Two archers both average the bullseye. One’s arrows land tightly together; the other’s are all over the target. Who would you trust? The one with the smaller spread.

Singkatnya: Sebaran (spread) mengukur seberapa jauh data berbeda satu sama lain. Sebaran kecil = konsisten; sebaran besar = bervariasi.

Formula

Range = Maximum − Minimum

Route A: 32 − 28 = 4 min  |  Route B: 40 − 15 = 25 min

⚠️ The weakness

Test scores: 60, 65, 70, 72, 75 → range = 75 − 60 = 15

One student was sick and scored 20: 20, 60, 65, 70, 72, 75

New range = 75 − 20 = 55. One value nearly quadrupled the range!

The range is like measuring a class by only looking at the shortest and tallest students. If one basketball player joins, the “class height range” suddenly looks huge, even though everyone else is the same.

Range uses only two values, so it is not resistant to outliers. That’s why we need better tools → next slide.

Singkatnya: Jangkauan = data terbesar − data terkecil. Mudah dihitung, tapi sangat terpengaruh pencilan.

Data (9 values): 2, 4, 5, 7, 8, 9, 12, 14, 16

1Median (Q₂) = 5th value = 8. It splits the data into a lower half and an upper half.
2Q₁ = median of the lower half (2, 4, 5, 7) = (4 + 5) ÷ 2 = 4.5
3Q₃ = median of the upper half (9, 12, 14, 16) = (12 + 14) ÷ 2 = 13
4IQR = Q₃ − Q₁ = 13 − 4.5 = 8.5 (the width of the middle 50%)

“Quart-” means four, like a quarter of a pizza. Q₁, Q₂, Q₃ are the three cuts that slice the sorted line into 4 groups, each with about 25% of the data.

Walk 3 steps forward and 3 steps back. Your position changes by 0, but you still walked 6 steps!

Deviations from the mean are the same: + and − cancel to 0. So we must remove the signs, either by taking the absolute value (→ MAD) or by squaring (→ SD).

Two ways to measure “typical distance”

MAD simpangan rata-rata: average of |x − mean|

SD simpangan baku: square, average, then square root. Far values count extra.

SAT tip: you will rarely compute SD by hand. You need to know what it means: the more the values spread away from the mean, the larger the SD.

Singkatnya: Simpangan baku (SD) ≈ jarak “khas” data dari mean. SD kecil = data rapat; SD besar = data menyebar. (SD sampel membagi dengan n − 1.)

SIMULATOR 6 • Shape explorer

Try: in “Skewed right,” the tallest bar is on the left, but it is called right-skewed. Why? Look at the tail!

Bimodal = two peaks, often two different groups mixed (e.g. heights of 7th graders + 12th graders). Uniform = all bars about equal.

Words to describe a graph

  • Peak: a high point. One peak = unimodal, two = bimodal.
  • Cluster: a crowded group of values.
  • Gap: an empty interval with no data.
  • Outlier: a value far from the rest.

The 1.5 × IQR rule (official outlier test)

A value is an outlier if it is

below Q₁ − 1.5 × IQR or above Q₃ + 1.5 × IQR

These two boundaries are called fences pagar. Anything outside the fence is “outside the yard.”

Example: 3, 4, 5, 5, 6, 6, 7, 8, 30

1Q₁ = 4.5, Q₃ = 7.5 → IQR = 3
21.5 × IQR = 1.5 × 3 = 4.5
3Fences: 4.5 − 4.5 = 0 and 7.5 + 4.5 = 12
430 > 12 → 30 is an outlier (red dot below)

Careful: finding an outlier does not explain why. Typing error? A real champion? Check the context.

SIMULATOR 7 • Bin explorer

How to read it

  • Line inside the box = median.
  • Box length = IQR (middle 50%).
  • Whisker ends = min and max (unless there are outliers).
  • Check the scale before comparing two plots.

⚠️ Common SAT traps

  • A longer section does NOT mean more data. It means the data there are more spread out. Each quarter still has ~25%.
  • A box plot cannot show how many values there are, or peaks.
  • A histogram cannot show exact values.
  • A graph that shows association does not prove causation.

Sentence frame

“Class ___ has a higher median, so its typical score is higher. Class ___ has a smaller IQR, so its middle scores are more consistent.”

Always (1) name the statistic and (2) say what it means in context.

Singkatnya: Bandingkan pusat (median), lalu sebaran (IQR/range), lalu bentuk & pencilan.

📈 Scatterplots: two variables at once

So far each data set had one variable. Now every person gives us two numbers, and we ask: do they move together?

Scatter diagramDirection, strength, formLine of best fitPrediction
📈
5

10 students: hours studied per week (x) and test score (y). The circled dot is a student who studied 5 hours and scored 77.

How to read it

  • x-axis (horizontal): the explanatory variable, the one we use to explain or predict.
  • y-axis (vertical): the response variable, the one we want to predict.
  • Each dot sits at the point (x, y) for one person or object.

A scatterplot is a map of pins. Each pin has two coordinates: how far across (x) and how far up (y). Look at the whole crowd of pins and ask: do they drift up, drift down, or scatter everywhere?

Singkatnya: Diagram pencar (scatterplot): satu titik = satu data berpasangan (x, y). Sumbu-x = variabel penjelas, sumbu-y = variabel yang diprediksi.
SIMULATOR 9 • Association explorer

30 points. Pick a pattern, then slide the noise (how scattered the points are) and watch the correlation coefficient r.

Lesson: r always lies between −1 and +1. The sign is the direction, the size (distance from 0) is the strength. Try “Curved”: r is near 0 even though the pattern is obvious, because r only measures straight-line association.

Singkatnya: r dekat +1 = naik kuat, r dekat −1 = turun kuat, r dekat 0 = tidak ada pola garis lurus. Pola melengkung bisa punya r ≈ 0.

Positive positif

As x ↑, y ↑.
Strong: points hug a line.

Negative negatif

As x ↑, y ↓.
Weaker: more scatter.

No visible pattern.
r ≈ 0.

Non-linear

A clear curve.

Ice cream and sunburn: both go up in summer, so their scatterplot shows a strong positive association. But ice cream does not cause sunburn; the hidden cause is sunny weather. Association ≠ causation.

Singkatnya: Deskripsikan: arah (positif/negatif), kekuatan (kuat/lemah), bentuk (lurus/melengkung), dan titik tak biasa. Hubungan ≠ sebab-akibat.

The red segments are residuals: the vertical gap between each actual dot and the line.

What “best” means

  • Draw a straight line through the cloud of points.
  • For each dot, measure the vertical miss (the residual).
  • The least-squares line is the one with the smallest total of squared residuals.

Roughly half the dots are above the line and half below. The line always passes through the point (x̄, ȳ), the mean of x and the mean of y.

Think of a bus route through a town: it cannot stop at every house, but it is placed so that, on average, everyone lives close to it.

Singkatnya: Garis regresi (garis terbaik) = garis lurus yang paling dekat ke semua titik. Selisih vertikal titik ke garis disebut residu.

SIMULATOR 12 • Equation builder

Move the two points. The line, slope, intercept and equation update. (Defaults: the points from Worked Problem 2.)

Three steps

1 Slope: m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)
2 Intercept: put one point into y = mx + b and solve: b = y₁ − m·x₁
3 Write it: ŷ = mx + b

Tip: the SAT often gives points on the line (read from the graph). Never use two random dots; dots are data, the line is the model.

Singkatnya: Persamaan garis dari dua titik: (1) m = (y₂ − y₁)/(x₂ − x₁), (2) b = y₁ − m·x₁, (3) tulis ŷ = mx + b.
The same 10 students

The two formulas

b = Σ(x − x̄)(y − ȳ) ÷ Σ(x − x̄)²

a = ȳ − b · x̄

Then the line is ŷ = a + bx. The top of the slope fraction measures how x and y move together; the bottom measures how much x spreads.

Think of the slope as “how much y moves, for the amount x moves”. If x-values are widely spread and y follows, the slope is steep.

Singkatnya: Hitung rata-rata x̄ dan ȳ → selisih tiap data dari rata-rata → kalikan & kuadratkan → b = Sxy/Sxx → a = ȳ − b·x̄.

A. From r and the standard deviations

b = r · (sy ÷ sx) then a = ȳ − b·x̄

Our data: r ≈ 0.965, sx ≈ 2.31, sy ≈ 11.57, so b ≈ 0.965 × (11.57 ÷ 2.31) ≈ 4.83. Same answer as by hand.

B. Use the point (x̄, ȳ)

The line passes through (x̄, ȳ), so once you know the slope:

ŷ − ȳ = b(x − x̄)

Our data: ŷ − 69.9 = 4.83(x − 4.3). Expand to get ŷ = 49.1 + 4.83x.

C. Desmos (built into the digital SAT)

  1. Click + → table. Type x-values in x₁ and y-values in y₁.
  2. On a new line type y₁ ~ m·x₁ + b
  3. Desmos shows m (slope), b (intercept) and r.

a = ȳ − b·x̄ = 50 − 3(10) = 20

Singkatnya: Jalan pintas: b = r·(sy/sx); garis selalu melalui (x̄, ȳ); di Desmos ketik y₁ ~ m·x₁ + b.
Line of best fit for the 10 students

ŷ = 49.1 + 4.83x

ŷ (“y-hat”) is the predicted test score. x is hours studied per week.
Slope 4.83: each extra hour of study goes with a predicted increase of about 4.83 points.
Intercept 49.1: the predicted score for 0 hours of study. (Only meaningful when x = 0 makes sense.)
Predict x = 6: ŷ = 49.1 + 4.83(6) ≈ 78.1. A student who studied 6 h actually scored 75, so residual = 75 − 78.1 = −3.1 (below the line).

Every residual in the data

The line passes through (x̄, ȳ) = (4.3, 69.9). The residuals add up to 0.

Singkatnya: Kemiringan = perubahan prediksi y setiap x naik 1. Residu = nilai sebenarnya − nilai prediksi. Titik di atas garis → residu positif.
SIMULATOR 11 • Prediction lab

Slide x to read the line’s prediction. The shaded band is where we actually have data. Then add one unusual student and watch the line.

Lesson: one unusual point can tilt the whole line, and predictions far outside the data (like 14 hours) can be impossible, such as a score above 100.

Singkatnya: Prediksi di dalam rentang data = interpolasi (cukup aman). Di luar rentang = ekstrapolasi (berisiko). Satu pencilan dapat mengubah garis.

1. Read the axes first

Name x, name y, and check the units. Most wrong answers swap the two variables.

2. Slope from the line

Pick two points on the line (not just dots). m = (y₂ − y₁) ÷ (x₂ − x₁)

3. Interpret in context

Slope: predicted change in y for each 1-unit increase in x. Intercept: predicted y when x = 0.

4. Residual

actual − predicted
Dot above the line → positive. Dot below the line → negative.

5. Choose the model

Linear: same difference each step. Exponential: same ratio each step. Quadratic: rises then falls (or the reverse).

6. Trust the prediction?

Inside the data range: reasonable. Outside it: extrapolation, be careful. And association never proves cause.

Singkatnya: Baca sumbu → hitung kemiringan dari dua titik pada garis → tafsirkan dalam konteks → residu = aktual − prediksi → pilih model yang tepat.
Problem 1

A line of best fit for a plant’s height y (cm) after x weeks is ŷ = 6 + 2.5x. At week 5 the plant measured 21 cm. What is the residual?

Predicted: ŷ = 6 + 2.5(5) = 18.5
Residual = actual − predicted = 21 − 18.5
+2.5 cm (the plant is above the line)
Problem 2

Two points on a line of best fit are (2, 10) and (8, 34). What does the line predict for x = 10?

Slope = (34 − 10) ÷ (8 − 2) = 24 ÷ 6 = 4
Intercept: 10 = 4(2) + b, so b = 2. The line is ŷ = 4x + 2.
ŷ(10) = 4(10) + 2 = 42
Problem 3

For x = 1, 2, 3, 4 the values of y are 3, 6, 12, 24. Which model fits best: linear, quadratic, or exponential?

Differences: 3, 6, 12. Not constant, so not linear.
Ratios: 6÷3 = 2, 12÷6 = 2, 24÷12 = 2. Constant!
Multiplying by the same factor each step → exponential

2. Adding a new value

The mean moves toward the new value. If the new value equals the mean, the mean stays the same.

3. Compare SD without calculating

The data set whose values sit farther from its mean has the larger SD.

4. Skew → mean vs median

Tail right: mean > median. Tail left: mean < median.

Problem 1

The mean of 6 test scores is 80. A 7th score is added and the mean becomes 82. What is the 7th score?

Old total = 6 × 80 = 480
New total = 7 × 82 = 574
7th score = 574 − 480 = 94
Problem 2

Data: 12, 15, 15, 18, 20, 22, 60. If 60 is removed, which changes more: the mean or the median?

Before: mean = 162 ÷ 7 ≈ 23.1; median = 18
After: mean = 102 ÷ 6 = 17; median = (15 + 18) ÷ 2 = 16.5
Mean drops ≈ 6.1, median only 1.5 → the mean
Problem 3

Class X: median 70, IQR 10. Class Y: median 70, IQR 25. Which statement must be true?

Same median → typical scores are the same.
IQR = width of the middle 50%. 10 < 25.
The middle half of class X is more consistent. (We cannot say anything certain about the means.)
